How to play

Three minutes of reading, then you're fine.

One gate

Two roads merge into a single trunk leading to your gate. If even one enemy reaches it, the run is over immediately. There is no health bar to spend.

Build

Pick an emplacement, then click clear ground. Roads, rocks and trees are off limits, and so is the hatched red ground near each spawn — you cannot camp the entrances.

Targeting

Every emplacement can be told what to shoot: first, last, toughest or closest. A Lance set to Toughest behaves completely differently from one set to First. This matters more than it sounds.

Contracts

Before each wave you're offered an optional contract — harder terms for better pay. You never have to sign. But the runs that go deep are the ones that signed early, while it was cheap.

Emplacements break

Every emplacement has health. Sappers detonate when killed. Wardens silence an emplacement for four seconds at a time. A damaged emplacement sells for less, so repair before you sell.

Armour

From wave five, every enemy subtracts a flat amount from each hit, growing as the waves go on. Ten cheap Bastions get gutted by it; one upgraded Lance barely notices. Upgrade what you have before you build more — that is the whole lesson.

What marches

Wraiths are fast and fragile. Brutes are slow and armoured. Broods split into two smaller copies when killed, twice over. Warded enemies shrug off 85% of all damage until a Cryo shot breaks the ward — nothing else can break it.

Warlords

Every fifth wave. A Warden silences your emplacements one at a time; a Herald continuously heals everything marching near it. Kill Heralds first or nothing else dies.

Curses

From wave four, a draft slot may hold a curse instead of a revision. Enormous gains, permanent costs. Blood Pact doubles your damage and ends repairs for the rest of the run.

Seals

Every run pays seals whether you win or lose. Spend them in the armoury on permanent upgrades that apply to every future run. That is what makes run ten different from run one.
